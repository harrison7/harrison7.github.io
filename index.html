Featured project

Elementary

An incremental game about building a star in a glass flask and collecting the periodic table. I'm designing and building it on my own.

In development · 2026

A star in a bottle

"Every atom has an origin story."

You start with an empty flask on a dark lab bench. Click to puff in hydrogen until it ignites, then keep the star fed, hot and contained as it fuses helium, carbon, oxygen and beyond. Each mechanic is a real nucleosynthesis process turned into a toy: the CNO cycle, helium shell burning, cosmic-ray spallation and proton capture.

The machine around the flask grows as you progress, and every new element gets its own discovery screen with its real spectral lines. Three acts are playable so far, from the first spark of hydrogen through six generations of stars.
